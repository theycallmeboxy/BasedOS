Your SD card, four seconds from power on.

BasedOS is a small base OS for the GKD 350H Ultra. It lives on the built-in storage next to the stock firmware, and its one job is to start spruce from your SD card, quickly and the same way every time.

Read the guide Releases

What you get

  • spruce's menu about 4 s after you press power.
  • Your spruce card stays an ordinary spruce card, so it still works in your other spruce devices.
  • Stock stays installed beside it. Hold Start at power-on for stock this once, or switch for good from stock's Ports.
  • A recovery card that puts the built-in storage back as it was, with no PC.
  • Nothing vendor-made in BasedOS: it uses your device's own stock firmware, in place, and never copies it.

Getting started

  1. Check the guide's What you need: the stock build, a spare SD card, a card for the recovery card.
  2. Download the setup card zip from a release and unpack it onto a spare SD card.
  3. Power on into stock with that card in, and open Ports > BasedOS.
  4. Choose Install BasedOS, and make the recovery card when it offers.
  5. Put your spruce card in and power on.
!

Pre-release. BasedOS is early and changes often. Read Recovery before you start, and keep the recovery card somewhere safe.
